How do I take a long screenshot?

Tap Scroll Capture, confirm screen sharing, then switch to the page you want and scroll. Stop with the system recording control or tap Stop & Stitch in FrameSplice. You can also make a screen recording in iOS and import it.

What if part of my screenshot is missing?

Tap the notice in the preview to check the affected area and review the original recording. When recording again, close overlays that cover the content and leave some overlap between successive views.
