The following information applies to Frame Splice 1.2. The interface and original recording retention may differ in earlier versions.

How do I take a long screenshot?

Tap Scroll Capture on the home screen and confirm screen sharing in the native iOS panel, then return to the page you want to capture. Wait for the system’s three-second countdown to finish and the red recording indicator to appear before scrolling. To finish, tap the system’s red recording indicator and confirm Stop, then open the app to view the result. Each recording keeps up to 20 seconds; capture longer content in sections.

You can also make a screen recording in iOS and import it. When you import a single recording, you can choose which clip to keep before generating an image.

What if part of my screenshot is missing?

Tap the notice in the preview to check the affected area. If an original recording is retained, you can review it or select a different clip. When recording again, close overlays that cover the content and leave some overlap between successive views.

How long are recordings and images kept?

After the finished image and editable source data are saved in History, temporary original recordings can be reclaimed automatically. Originals needed for further editing stay with the project. Failed recordings or recordings awaiting review can be viewed, retried or deleted in Recordings to Review. Recovery copies use a 24-hour window; expired copies are removed automatically when the app is idle and the files are not in use.

History uses a 1 GB storage budget for project files. Near that budget, the app automatically removes older projects when cleanup can run, while protecting projects in use. Save important images separately to Photos. You can also delete projects manually from History. Deletion or automatic cleanup in the app does not affect exported copies; manage those separately in Photos or the chosen sharing destination.
