The following information applies to Frame Splice 1.2. The interface and original recording retention may differ in earlier versions.

FrameSplice is an iPhone tool for long screenshots and image stitching. It requires no account and does not collect or upload screen recordings, screenshots, stitched images, editing history, or device usage data. It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking services.

Screen recording and Photos

FrameSplice records only after you initiate capture and confirm screen sharing in the native iOS panel. Return to the page you want to capture, and wait for the system’s three-second countdown to finish and the red recording indicator to appear before scrolling. Sharing the entire screen may include other apps and visible notifications in the recording. Tap the system’s red recording indicator and confirm Stop to end the recording. FrameSplice does not record microphone or system audio.

When you import media, FrameSplice accesses only files you select in the system picker. When importing a single screen recording, you can select a clip before generating an image. When you save or share, the resulting image is written to Photos or sent to the destination you choose. Photos and sharing destinations may sync or store copies according to their settings.

Local storage and deletion

Recording and editing take place on your device. After the finished image and editable source data are saved in History, temporary original recordings can be reclaimed automatically. Originals needed for further editing stay with the project. Failed recordings or recordings awaiting review can be viewed, retried or deleted in Recordings to Review. Recovery copies use a 24-hour window; expired copies are removed automatically when the app is idle and the files are not in use.

History uses a 1 GB storage budget for project files. Near that budget, the app automatically removes older projects when cleanup can run, while protecting projects in use. Save important images separately to Photos. You can also delete projects manually from History. Deletion or automatic cleanup in the app does not affect exported copies; manage those separately in Photos or the chosen sharing destination.
