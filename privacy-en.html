FrameSplice

Privacy Policy

简体中文 · English · 繁體中文 · 日本語

FrameSplice is an iPhone tool for long screenshots and image stitching. It requires no account and does not collect or upload screen recordings, screenshots, stitched images, editing history, or device usage data. It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking services.

Screen recording and Photos

FrameSplice records only after you initiate capture and confirm screen sharing in iOS. When you share your entire screen, the recording may include other apps and visible notifications. Stop using the iOS recording control or the in-app stop button. FrameSplice does not record microphone or system audio.

When you import media, FrameSplice accesses only files you select in the system picker. When you save or share, the resulting image is written to Photos or sent to the destination you choose. Photos and sharing destinations may sync or store copies according to their settings.

Local storage and deletion

Recording and editing take place on your device. FrameSplice keeps your latest original recording and stitched images so you can review or edit them. Delete the original recording from its menu on the home screen, or remove a stitched image from History. Manage previously exported copies separately in Photos or the chosen sharing destination.
